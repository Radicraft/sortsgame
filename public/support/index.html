Getting started

How do I play?

Swipe your finger across touching letter blocks to trace a word, up, down, left or right (no diagonals, but you can turn corners). Lift your finger and any real word of three letters or more scores automatically. Scored blocks burst and the board falls and refills. When a coloured block touches others of the same colour, the whole cluster explodes for bonus points.

Can I see the lessons again?

Yes. Open Settings (the gear at the top of the home screen) and tap Replay the tutorial. It runs the lessons on a practice board, then walks you round the home and the book's map.

What are pages?

Pages are how you score each level, up to three a level. Each book has four chapters, and each chapter is sealed by a page gate: collect enough pages and you break the seal by hand. Replay earlier levels any time to earn more.

Why do some levels have a wax seal?

Those are Hard and Super Hard levels, with less time or fewer words and higher page marks. Elsewhere, a level's first and last pages rise as your play gets stronger, and after a loss the first page eases for your next try. Prefer every level to ask for the book's own pages? Switch off Pages follow how you play in Settings. Hard levels stay hard either way.

What is the Bindery?

Every page you earn also goes into your purse. Spend pages in the Bindery on titles, inks and tile styles for your character. Pages are never sold, and spending them never closes a chapter gate you have already opened.

What's free & The Full Library

What is free?

Books One and Two (the first 200 levels), the seasonal book, the Daily Edition, duels, friends, guilds and the Forbidden Section, plus three Endless runs and one Compositor's Cabinet bed every day. There are no ads, for anyone.

What does The Full Library add?

One purchase, yours for good: Books Three to Six and every campaign book added later, unlimited Endless and Compositor's Cabinet, the premium track of every season ledger, and the Patron's Set (a title, a frame for your sort, an ink and tiles of your own). Find it in Settings, or tap a sealed book under Your books on the home screen. It is not a subscription, so there is nothing to renew or cancel.

I played before version 1.4. Do I need to buy it?

No. Anyone who downloaded SORTS before version 1.4, and anyone who ever held a Pressroom Pass, gets The Full Library at no charge, with a Founding Reader title. If it isn't showing, tap Restore Purchases in Settings.

What happened to coins and the Pressroom Pass?

Version 1.4 retired both. Coin balances were turned into pages at one page for every ten coins, rounded up, and everything you bought with coins (books read early, the Compositor's Cabinet, the Midnight Ledger's premium page, skins, inks and titles) stays yours. If an old Pressroom Pass renewal still appears under your Apple Account's subscriptions, you can cancel it there: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions.

How do I restore my purchases?

Open Settings in SORTS and tap Restore Purchases, or tap Restore on The Full Library sheet. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they return on any device signed in with it. Items you earned or bought with pages or coins come back from your iCloud save in the same step.

Can my family share The Full Library?

Not at the moment: Family Sharing is switched off for this purchase, so it opens the library for the Apple Account that bought it.

A purchase didn't go through, or I'd like a refund.

Try Restore Purchases first. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. If something still looks wrong, email hello@sortsgame.com with the date and what you bought and we'll sort it out.

Guilds & friends

How do I join a guild?

Tap Guild in the bar at the bottom of the home screen. You can find an open guild with a free seat, start your own, or join with a guild's code, which any member can share from their guild screen.

How do guilds work?

Up to five players pool the points they score each week toward a shared goal. Hit the goal to claim rewards and climb the weekly Guild League, and earn enough together to open that week's Forbidden Section volume. Points reset every Monday.

How do I add friends?

Swap friend codes, or use In the Room to find players nearby without typing anything. In the Room and same-room duels use your local network, so iOS asks permission the first time.

Reporting a guild or player

Guild names and profiles are moderated, and you can report or block other players from their profile or the guild screen. To report abuse, use the in-app report option or email hello@sortsgame.com.

Devices, sound & accessibility

Does SORTS work on iPad and iPhone Duo?

Yes. On iPad the home and every level open as a two page book. On iPhone Duo, open the phone mid-level and the same board carries on as a two page spread with the spine on the hinge, and fold it again to keep playing on the cover screen.

Can I turn the sound, music or vibration off?

Yes. Sound, Music and Haptics each have their own switch in Settings. Music also stays quiet while another app is playing audio.

What accessibility options are there?

Colour-blind shapes give each tile value its own shape, so chains don't rely on colour. Relaxed clock plays the books' timed levels against a number of words instead of a clock. SORTS also follows the system's VoiceOver, Switch Control, Dynamic Type, Bold Text, Increase Contrast and Reduce Motion settings.

Account & data

Do I need an account?

No sign-up is required. Your library is backed up to iCloud, so a new phone picks up where you left off. Social features (guilds, leaderboards, friends) use Apple Game Center and iCloud, so signing in to those on your device lets friends see your name, character and scores.

Can I play offline?

Yes. The books, the Daily Edition and same-room duels all play with no internet, and your progress syncs when you are next online.

Privacy & data deletion

SORTS shows no ads and uses no third-party tracking. See our Privacy Policy for what's handled and how. To request a copy or deletion of your player-profile data, email hello@sortsgame.com.

Troubleshooting

  • Progress or guild not syncing: make sure you're signed in to iCloud and Game Center in your device Settings, then relaunch. SORTS Settings shows whether iCloud backup is on.
  • The board feels stuck: use Free Shuffle for a fresh board, or wait a moment and the board glows a word you can trace.
  • Can't find a player In the Room: check that SORTS is allowed Local Network access in the iOS Settings app, and that Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are switched on for both phones.
  • Something looks wrong: tell us the device and iOS version at hello@sortsgame.com and we'll take a look.
