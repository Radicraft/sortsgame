Getting started

How do I play?

Swipe your finger across touching letter blocks to trace a word, up, down, left or right (no diagonals, but you can turn corners). Lift your finger and any real word of three letters or more scores automatically. Scored blocks burst and the board falls and refills. When a coloured block touches others of the same colour, the whole cluster explodes for bonus points.

What are pages?

Pages are how you score each level (up to three per level). Collect enough pages to open the next sealed chapter of a book. You can replay earlier levels any time to earn more.

What's the difference between pages and coins?

Pages unlock story chapters and can't be bought; you earn them by playing. Coins are the in-game currency for boosters and cosmetics, earned through play and daily rewards, or bought as optional coin packs.

Purchases & the Pressroom Pass

How do I restore my purchases?

Open The Desk (settings) and tap Restore Purchases. Your purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so they'll return on any device signed in with it.

How do I manage or cancel the Pressroom Pass?

The Pressroom Pass is an auto-renewing subscription managed by Apple. To view, change or cancel it: open the Settings app on your device, tap your name at the top, then Subscriptions, then SORTS. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep access until the current period ends.

A purchase didn't go through, or I was charged but didn't receive coins.

First try Restore Purchases (above). If coins or a subscription still don't appear, email us at hello@sortsgame.com with the date and what you bought and we'll sort it out.

Guilds & friends

How do I join a guild?

Tap the Guild button, choose Join, and enter the guild's code. Ask a member to send you their code; they'll find it on their guild screen and can share it from the invite button.

How do guilds work?

Everyone in a guild pools their weekly word scores toward a shared goal. Hit the goal to earn rewards and climb the weekly Guild League. Scores reset every Monday.

Reporting a guild or player

Guild names are moderated, and you can report or block other players from the guild screen. To report abuse, use the in-app report option or email hello@sortsgame.com.

Account & data

Do I need an account?

No sign-up is required. Social features (guilds, leaderboards, friends) use Apple Game Center and iCloud, so signing in to those on your device lets friends see your name, character and scores.

Privacy & data deletion

SORTS shows no ads and uses no third-party tracking. See our Privacy Policy for what's handled and how. To request a copy or deletion of your player-profile data, email hello@sortsgame.com.

Troubleshooting

  • Progress or guild not syncing: make sure you're signed in to iCloud and Game Center in your device Settings, then relaunch.
  • The board feels stuck: use Free Shuffle for a fresh board, or wait a moment and the board glows a word you can trace.
  • Something looks wrong: tell us the device and iOS version at hello@sortsgame.com and we'll take a look.
