Why you'll get hooked

Pick up in sixty seconds. Or lose an hour. Your call.

Drag your finger across touching letter blocks, up, down, left or right, turning corners as you go. Lift off and any real word of three letters or more scores instantly. Scored blocks smash apart, the board falls like candy, and matching inks chain into cascades with bigger and bigger bonuses the longer they run. There's sound and music now too, and your phone answers every hit.

Six engraved books

600 hand-tuned levels, a hundred to a book. Every book is a world you walk through, with your own little sort on the road and a page gate sealing each chapter.

The Patchwork Man

The seasonal book, a cosy retelling after Mary Shelley's Frankenstein. A hundred more levels, free for everyone, with jelly letters wobbling in his cauldron.

The Daily Edition

One puzzle every morning, the same for everybody. Keep your streak, claim the daily gift, and see how your friends got on.

The Compositor's Cabinet

A letterpress match-3. Line up three of one ink, then print the best word your cleared letters can spell for double score.

Duels across the table

Two phones, one table, no internet needed. Sixty seconds on the same board, a rematch on the spot, and a rivalry record to defend.

The Book With No Last Page

Endless mode: a countdown you push back with every word you score. How long can you last?

Wax-sealed levels

Some levels wear a seal. Hard and Super Hard ones ask a lot, and the rest of each book sets its pages by how you play.

Sound you can feel

Recorded sound and music, with haptics timed to every block that smashes. Sound, music and haptics each have their own switch.

Made for iPhone Duo

Open the phone mid-level and the board becomes a two page book, carrying on without a new deal. On iPad, every level is an open book too.

After hours

What the guilds keep.

Five hands to a guild. Weekly points pooled toward a shared goal, the finest words shelved in a Commonplace Book, and a League that remembers everything.

  • The Guild Library. The finest words your guild sets are shelved together, credited in ink to whoever pulled them, beside every volume you have bound.
  • The Guild League. Tier by tier, guild against guild, every week.
  • The Forbidden Section. A restricted volume appears each week. It opens for no single player. Your guild earns it together, or not at all.

Behind the velvet rope

Every volume, bound.

Each week a restricted volume waits behind the rope, and it drops when your guild's points climb high enough. Clear it and it's bound onto your guild's shelf for good, beside the crests you strike along the way.
