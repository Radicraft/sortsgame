Why you'll get hooked

Pick up in sixty seconds. Or lose an hour. Your call.

Drag your finger across touching letter blocks in any direction, turning corners as you go. Lift off and any real word of three letters or more scores instantly. Scored blocks burst, the board falls like candy, and matching colours chain into cascades with bigger and bigger bonuses the longer they run.

Three illustrated books

300 hand-tuned levels along a hand-drawn story trail, a hundred to a book. Every level you clear turns another page.

The Compositor's Cabinet

A letterpress match-3, new to SORTS. Match the inks, set your stick, and print the word.

The Daily Edition

A fresh printed puzzle every day, with build-a-streak rewards, daily gifts and quests.

Same-room duels

Two phones, one table, sixty seconds. No accounts to add, just tap in, race a friend, and build a rivalry record.

Endless Mode

A countdown you extend with every word you score. How long can you last?

What the guilds keep.

Five hands to a guild. Weekly scores pooled toward a shared goal, finest words shelved in a Commonplace Book, and a League that remembers everything.

  • The Guild Library. The finest words your guild sets are shelved together, credited in ink to whoever pulled them.
  • The Guild League. Division by division, guild against guild, every week.
  • The Forbidden Section. A restricted volume appears each week. It opens for no single player. Your guild earns it together, or not at all.
